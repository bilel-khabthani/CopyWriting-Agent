# Good brief guide (read this before generating)

The tool **mirrors your brief**. A one-line brief produces weak, invented
output; a rich brief produces strong, concrete copy. This is the biggest
quality lever you control.

## The flow

1. **Pick a category**, then a **format** (Video Ad, Landing Page, Online Ad,
   Email / Newsletter, ...).
2. **Answer the common questions** (same for every format, below).
3. **Answer the format-specific questions** (only some formats ask these).
4. Tap **Generate**. The output shape follows the format you chose.

## The common questions, field by field

1. **Brand name** - the product or brand, or `-` if none.
2. **Product description** - say what it *is*: category, material, function. Not "a product that sells X".
3. **Features / benefits** - 2-3 concrete things: materials, what makes it unique, price, warranty.
4. **Audience** - who they are, their age range, and their situation.
5. **Goal / Tone** - picked from the buttons (Video Ad also asks platform + product type).

## Format-specific questions

| Format | Extra question |
|---|---|
| Online Ad | Which channels? (FB/IG, Google Search...) |
| Email / Newsletter | What should the email do? |
| Social Post | Which network? (LinkedIn, Instagram...) |
| Paid / Private Newsletter | Who reads it? (members, subscribers, customers) |
| Landing Page | What is the main offer or promise? |
| E-commerce Product Sheet | Paste the key specs to convert into benefits |
| Podcast Script | What is the episode about, and who is the host/guest? |
| Flyer / Brochure | Contact info / address / QR to include |
| Poster / Slogan | Where will it be displayed? |
| Sales Letter | What is the offer, and what action should the reader take? |
| Video Ad | Platform + product type (built into the flow) |

## Bad vs good (real example)

**Bad input**
> Produit qui vent des nouveaux models de crampons

**Good input**
> **Product:** Des crampons de football légers pour jeunes joueurs : tige en mesh respirant, semelle à crampons moulés pour terrain sec, design coloré à la mode. Prix 45 €.
> **Features:** mesh respirant, semelle moulée pour terrain sec, design coloré, 45 €.
> **Audience:** jeunes garçons de 12 à 22 ans qui jouent au foot en club ou dans la rue.

Same product - but now the model has real material and invents nothing.

## Checklist before you tap Generate
- [ ] The product **category** is explicit (e.g. "crampons", not "produit")
- [ ] 2-3 **concrete features**
- [ ] **Audience** with an age range
- [ ] Anything **unique** (price, warranty, made in, guarantee)
- [ ] The **format-specific** answer filled in (channels, offer, specs, contact...)
- [ ] Spelling roughly correct (helps, but not required)

## Tips
- If the client has a product page, **copy/paste** the description and specs.
- Mention a **price or offer** when relevant.
- If you don't have the info, ask the client before generating - it costs one message.
